About

Lauren Bush is a creator and host who can write it, shoot it, carry it on camera and then explain why it worked.

She built Rally Off Court, a pickleball lifestyle brand with a real audience, and produces the content herself. That means she shows up already knowing how a segment gets made, how a sponsor wants to be seen and how long a viewer will actually stay. Before that she led a $5M+ communications portfolio at The Hatcher Group, including work for the Rockefeller Foundation Pandemic Prevention Institute and AHRQ TeamSTEPPS. She has briefed federal clients, run national campaigns and stood in front of rooms that ask hard questions.

On set she is warm, quick and unbothered. She reads a room, keeps a segment moving, makes a guest comfortable and lands the brand line without sounding like a commercial. She is equally at home in an unscripted television format, a product explainer, a brand activation or a keynote room.
